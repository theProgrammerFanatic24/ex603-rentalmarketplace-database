# Unit 4 Finding: The True No-Show Rate

**To:** Leadership team
**Re:** Actual failure rate and which properties to investigate

## The true failure rate

Out of 200 property viewings on the platform, **12 were renter no-shows, a failure rate of about 6%**. This replaces the dashboard's earlier figure, which overstated the problem.

The no-shows are spread thinly. No single property has more than 3, and 4 of the 13 properties had none at all.

## How it varies across properties

Most properties sit close to or below the platform rate. Properties with steady activity do better: the 8 properties with more than 15 viewings have a combined no-show rate of **4.8%**, below the platform's 6%. The overall rate is pushed up by a few properties with fewer viewings.

By region, the picture is uneven:

| City | Viewings | No-shows | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Danang | 16 | 3 | 19% |
| Haiphong | 50 | 4 | 8% |
| Hue | 36 | 2 | 6% |
| Saigon | 62 | 2 | 3% |
| Hanoi | 36 | 1 | 3% |
| **Platform** | **200** | **12** | **6%** |

Danang stands out at roughly three times the platform rate, even though it has the fewest viewings.

## Properties to investigate

1. **Property 2:** the most no-shows on the platform (3 of 16 viewings, about 19%), more than three times the platform rate on a meaningful number of viewings.
2. **Property 3:** the second-most no-shows (2 of 13 viewings, about 15%), and the shortest average visit (13 minutes) among the properties with the most no-shows, suggesting something beyond no-shows is affecting it.
3. **Property 4 (watch, not yet act):** a high rate (1 of 6 viewings) but too few viewings to draw a firm conclusion. Worth monitoring as more viewings come in.

## A note on the numbers

No-shows are only part of the story. All 34 cancelled viewings, for any reason, are recorded as 0 minutes long, so cancellations for other reasons (renter cancelled, agent unavailable, property withdrawn) also pull down how long viewings appear to last. Property 11, for example, has the shortest average visit of any property but only one no-show. Its other cancellations are worth a look as a separate question.
